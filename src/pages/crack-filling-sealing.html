Why crack filling matters here

In the high desert, cracks don't stay small. Freeze-thaw cycles in winter pry them wider, and summer monsoon storms push water through them into the base underneath. Once the base softens, the surface starts to sink, break up and pothole.

Filling cracks early is one of the least expensive things you can do for your pavement — and it makes a sealcoat last longer when you're ready for one.

How we do it

  • Walk the surface with you and measure
  • Sweep and clean debris out of the cracks
  • Fill and seal with a product suited to the crack and the surface
  • Work only in dry weather and the right temperatures
  • Leave it clean and neat, then follow up to see how it's holding
