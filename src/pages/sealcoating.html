What sealcoating does

Fresh asphalt is black and flexible. Years of intense high-desert sun oxidize it — it dries out, fades to gray and gets brittle, and brittle pavement cracks. A sealcoat slows that process down and keeps water from soaking in.

As a general guideline, most pavement benefits from sealcoating every 2–3 years, depending on traffic and how much sun it gets. Vincent will look at yours and give you an honest answer.

Our sealcoating process

  • Sweep and clean the surface
  • Fill cracks first so the coat isn't just covering them up
  • Apply sealcoat evenly, edge to edge
  • Schedule around dry weather and the right temperatures — we'll call ahead if the forecast turns
  • For businesses, schedule around your operating hours

Parking lot? Add striping after the sealcoat for crisp, visible lines.
