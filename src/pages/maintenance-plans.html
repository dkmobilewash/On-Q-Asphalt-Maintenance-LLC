A simple upkeep rhythm

As a general guideline — not a promise, since every surface is different — most asphalt in our area does well with:

  • Crack filling whenever new cracks open up
  • Sealcoating every 2–3 years, depending on traffic and sun
  • Prompt repairs for potholes and broken areas
  • Restriping parking lots when lines fade

Want us to keep track of it for you? Ask about scheduling regular upkeep — several of our customers come back every couple of years.
