Built for high-desert pavement

We know what Rio Rancho and Albuquerque weather does to asphalt

Our intense sun and UV slowly oxidize asphalt, drying it out until it fades to gray and starts to crack. Winter freeze-thaw cycles then widen any crack that's left open, and summer monsoon storms push water into those cracks and under the pavement.

That's why the basics matter: fill cracks early to keep water out, and sealcoat to protect the surface from the sun. As a general guideline, plan on crack filling as needed and sealcoating every 2–3 years, depending on traffic and sun exposure — Vincent will tell you what your pavement actually needs.

FAQ

Common questions

Are your quotes free?

Yes. Vincent comes out, looks at the job with you, measures it and emails you a written quote. There's no obligation.

How much does sealcoating or crack filling cost?

It depends on the size of the area, how many cracks there are and what condition the pavement is in — which is why we measure before we quote. What we can tell you: keeping up with maintenance costs less than waiting until the pavement fails.

Why did you reschedule because of the weather?

Sealcoat and crack sealant need dry weather and the right temperatures to cure properly. If it's cold or drizzling, we'll call ahead and move your job rather than do it poorly.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes — veterans and active military, just let us know when you request your quote. Learn more.
