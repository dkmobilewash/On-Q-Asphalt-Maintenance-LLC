Keeping it up

Many of our driveway customers come back for regular upkeep. As a general guideline, a driveway benefits from a fresh seal every 2–3 years and crack filling whenever new cracks open up — less if it's shaded and lightly used, more if it bakes in the sun all day.

Regular sealing costs far less than repairing a driveway that's been left to break down.
