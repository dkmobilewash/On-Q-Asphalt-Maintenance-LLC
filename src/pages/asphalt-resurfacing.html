When resurfacing makes sense

  • Widespread cracking across most of the surface
  • The surface is rough, raveling or badly faded
  • Repeated patches that keep failing
  • You want a clean, uniform look for your home or business

Straight answers first

Resurfacing isn't always the answer, and we won't sell it to you if a repair and sealcoat will do the job. We'll walk the surface with you, measure it, and explain your options in a written quote.

If there are any issues along the way, we'll tell you right away — and once it's done, keep it protected with sealcoating and crack filling down the road.
